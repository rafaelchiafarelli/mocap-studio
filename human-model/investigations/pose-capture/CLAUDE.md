Gains are `--gain` times the region's, 0 to 3 (Rafael: past 3 the take is lost). Writes OUT.json: per part, motion and noise rms before and after, and motion by axis (x tilt, y roll, z turn for pelvis, chest, head).

- `foot_contacts.py`: on a two-camera `.npz` (needs `points_m` and the `two_cameras.json` beside it): finds Rafael's mark (a VLC snapshot matched against every frame of both videos, the frame as stored, no rotation; or seconds in A), fits one plane through every still ankle, heel and toe point (one height per point, points far above it dropped as feet paused in the air) and takes that ramp out of every point's height (`level_shear`: x, y and vertical lines kept); the marked foot's points, averaged over +-0.1 s, give each point's standing height, the same for both feet, its lowest at z = 0; a point is on the floor within `--contact-mm` of its standing height and under `--speed`, runs under 3 frames dropped. Writes the same keys (points_m levelled, world recomputed) plus `contact` (T, 2, 3) [left, right] x [ankle, heel, toe], `floor_heights_m`, `floor_ramp`, `floor_shift_m`; OUT.json (ramp, mark, contacts per point, how deep each point goes under its height) and OUT.png (heights before and after, contacts). Warns over 30 mm of plane residual (a camera moved, or the reconstruction bends the floor).

## Key facts / gotchas

- The 'heavy' model (complexity 2) is downloaded by MediaPipe at run time into site-packages, which the image's user can't write: `capture_pose.py` uses 'full' (`COMPLEXITY`).
- Clip 0100: front pose from frame 1 to ~250, a turn around ~320, then the back. MediaPipe keeps left and right right in the back (its trunk turns to face away), but its depth is poor there: the hip line shrinks from 0.23 m to ~0.11 m.
- Axes: MediaPipe (x right, y down, z away) → Blender (x, z, -y), a proper rotation, so left stays left; `drive_capture.py` checks it per frame (`side_check_failures`, empty on clip 0100).
- `pb.matrix` writes leave rounding scale on the bone; `drive_capture.py` resets `pb.scale` each frame, or it grows (the first run's left toe climbed 1.5 m; that `.blend` was overwritten by the fixed run).
- The woman wears high heels and the body is barefoot, so captured feet point down.
- The captured woman's height is taken as 1.70 m (Rafael, 2026-11-14); Rafael's body is 1.59 m. Retargeting by direction doesn't depend on either.
- Two views of points alone can't tell the focal length (a wrong one reprojects as well); `two_cameras.py` fits it with the limb bones held rigid. Synthetic median 3D error for 1.5 / 5 / 10 px of 2D noise: 6 / 20 / 43 mm (`calibrate` docstring). MediaPipe's own 2D noise is what limits the result.
- Phone videos: variable frame rate (so `capture_pose.py` stores each frame's time), a rotation flag (size taken from a decoded frame), and audio and video streams that don't start together (`meta.json` from `ffprobe`). The clips in `passarela_trechos/clips/` have no sound.
- Smoke test 2026-11-14 with clip 0100 against itself shifted 0.3 s: sync found -0.300 s; with music and no clap the confidence was 1.28, under the 1.5 that prints a warning.
- OpenCV in the reference image doesn't apply a phone video's rotation by default (`CAP_PROP_ORIENTATION_AUTO` is 0): MediaPipe then saw the person lying down. `capture_pose.py` and `two_cameras.py` turn it on.
- The rigid-skeleton fit starts each frame from that frame's triangulation; started from the last fit, it fell behind the motion (error grew from 11 to 200 px in 40 frames). It needs `fit_sparsity`, or a run takes over 10 minutes.
- On the 2026-11-14 videos: triangulation alone left limb bones varying 20–30% in length; with the skeleton fit on 31.46–42.85 s, 2–13%, with 12 px median reprojection in each view. Only the body inside both pictures counts: in 70% of frames it is, with visibility not required (`both_views.py`).
- Skin waves on the captured dance (`data/skin_waves.json` as tuned for the walk): peaks 35 mm breast, 17 glute, 14 thigh, 10 flank, 7 belly; 266 substeps per frame at 30 fps. The capture's own noise also drives them.
- Rafael (2026-11-14): the dance had more hip motion than MediaPipe caught. Its pelvis is two hip points under the shorts, so tilt and roll of the pelvis are barely seen; the capture's smoothing (2 frames) and the skeleton's stillness term damp further.
- Motion measured on `06_` at gain 1 (`13_ganho/ganho_1.json`, rms of the motion band): pelvis 10.0° (tilt 1.5, roll 2.7, turn 9.5), chest on pelvis 17.1° (mostly turn), arms 13–15°, legs 7–11°, hips' path 71 mm; noise 4° pelvis, 8–11° chest and head (B cuts the head off). The pelvis's tilt and roll are the barely-seen ones, so gain 3 brings roll to ~8°. With hips gain 3 the hips travel 0.75 × 0.92 m against 0.59 × 0.69 m at 2 (`stage_video.py`).
- Gain scales the motion band only: noise slower than 6 Hz can't be told from motion and is amplified with it; at 30 fps per-frame noise spreads up to 15 Hz, so `--noise 0` removes a bit over half of it. The bands overlap: a 1.5 Hz sway is 96% motion, a 0.5 Hz one 85%.
- The floor of a capture is always level (Rafael, 2026-11-14). On `06_` the still foot points nonetheless lie on a ramp of 9.9° (172 mm per m in depth, 30 in width; residual 14 mm), while the body's ankles-to-shoulders line is upright (median lean -0.1°): the reconstruction shears height with depth (likely the focal length or camera height the two views can't pin down, see the focal gotcha), it isn't turned. Turning the capture levelled the floor and leaned the body 9.8° (`14_..._ERRADO_`); the shear levels it and keeps the lean at -0.1°. The ramp differs per take, so `foot_contacts.py` measures it on each one; the cause in `two_cameras.py`'s calibration is not fixed. Levelled, Rafael's sneakers stand with ankle 73 mm, heel 7 mm, toe 0 mm; one frame near A 35.8 s throws the right foot 90–160 mm under the floor (a tracking spike).
- MPFB's trunk and limbs have only ~4,000 skin vertices (head, hands and feet hold the rest), a vertex every ~2 cm: the shown skin is MPFB's subdivided twice (~64,000), tied to the 3 mm tetrahedra.
- The bands' glue diverged with zero compliance: a glue whose only free node had a weight of 3e-15 divided by ~1e-25 (`tissue_solver.py`, `GLUE_COMPLIANCE`).
- Built in MPFB's A pose, arms brought to the sides folded armpit and shoulder tissue onto itself: thousands of tetrahedra inverted and grooves opened down the flank. Built in the dance's first frame (`pose=`), none invert at the start; zones, groups and fat thickness are still read per vertex at rest, and the trunk's rules through the rest frame (`cr`), including breast side.
- Arm and trunk are split by geometry (`ARM_RADIUS_M`), not MPFB's weights, which gave the upper arm much of the chest's side; shoulders have a rigid capsule standing for clavicle and scapula; the rib cage is one rigid piece on spine.003; nodes damp against the nearest driven node's bone; only voxels inside the skin are kept (outside ones bridged arm and flank).
- Measured (malha_v6, 3.34 M tetrahedra, 160 steps): 242 ms per frame, 341 frames in ~1.5 min; volume kept 99.8–100%; 0 inverted at the start, up to ~6,000 (0.2%) in the dance. Steps 160, 320 and 640 gave the same motion (converged). Breasts 1.22 L each; under gravity they sag ~25 mm from MPFB's shape (which is already the standing shape: no pre-stress yet) and swing up to ~50 mm.

- Open (2026-11-14): thin creases still show on the back (a diagonal from the shoulder), the side of the hip and the belly in some frames; not yet located.
