# 2. One check when the design is finished

Bones are calculated once, and one check says all is well (Rafael, 2026-11-08).

## Contract

**In:** a finished character.

**Out:**
- A button that builds the bones once and checks the body at rest: no bone through the skin, proportions within the silhouette's ranges, the percentage reached. Written as a short report; it warns and doesn't block.

Task 1, and epic `shapes`.
