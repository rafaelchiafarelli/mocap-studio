# 3. The cup, both ways

Setting the cup holds the thorax and raises the bust; setting the bust recalculates the cup.

## Contract

**In:** sub-busto, busto or cup in a character.

**Out:**
- In `resolve`: cup from bust minus sub-busto, at 2 cm per letter; a typed cup sets the bust; a typed bust sets the cup; a changed sub-busto with the cup set moves the bust.
- The letters and the difference for the first one in `data/cup_table.json`, editable. Tests both ways.

## Agreed and declared

- 2 cm per letter (Rafael, 2026-11-08). The first letter's difference, 12 to 14 cm for A as in the European convention, is declared and can be edited.
