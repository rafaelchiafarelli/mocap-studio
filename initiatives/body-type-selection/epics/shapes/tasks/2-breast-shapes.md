# 2. The breast's shapes and adjustments

Four exclusive shapes and three independent adjustments.

## Contract

**In:** the divided zones, the gland and the cup.

**Out:**
- Redondo, gota, cônico and tuberoso as editable numbers in `data/breast_shapes.json`; espaçamento, assimetria (per side) and queda (position and the skin's elasticity) apply to any of them. Volume, density and the rest stay adjustable.

## Dependencies

Epic `volumes-outward`.

## Pre-work

None.
