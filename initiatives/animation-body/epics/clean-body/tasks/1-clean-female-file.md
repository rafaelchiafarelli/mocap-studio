# 1. A clean woman from sliders alone

The base of everything after it: no bones, muscles, balloon or pushed skin, so the shape at rest is what MPFB2 gives.

## Contract

**In:** a recipe in `data/recipes/` (macros and targets).

**Out:**
- `blender/clean_body.py`: an MPFB2 woman from a recipe, with its own rig, the helpers masked and the skin subdivided on display.
- The skin keeps its 13,380 body vertices, in MPFB's order, and no later step edits vertices before the sliders.
- A new `.blend` per attempt, under a name that says what it holds, in `G:\mocap-studio-downloads\review\<date>\`.

## Agreed and declared

- Rest shape judged first by Rafael: his complaint was skin "quadrada", dents and a hollow above the breast (2026-11-10).
