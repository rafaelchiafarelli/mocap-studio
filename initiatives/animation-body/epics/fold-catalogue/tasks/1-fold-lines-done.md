# 1. The catalogue of folds

Folds sit where skin is tied down, so they can be declared rather than simulated.

## Contract

**In:** the clean body's 13,380 vertices.

**Out:**
- `data/folds.json`: per fold, its name, side, the skin vertices of its line, the joint that drives it, and its depth by eye.
- At least: under the buttock, under the breast, armpit, inner elbow, back of the knee, side of the waist, belly.

## Agreed and declared

- A fold not in the catalogue does not exist: accepted (2026-11-10).

## Dependencies

Epic `pose-corrections`.

## Pre-work

None.
