# tissue-research

**Goal:** replace the body tissue's declared values with measured ones (Rafael, 2026-11-07). The thigh experiment (`muscle-physics`, epic `thigh`) starts with one muscle volume and fat thickness set by eye; this initiative finds the data that should replace them.

**Status:** to be planned. Nothing here starts before the thigh experiment's verdict.

## Questions to research

- **Fat thickness per zone,** by sex and build, in the zones plastic surgery uses: from CT tissue labels (TotalSegmentator appears to have a tissue task; its license must allow commercial use) or published measurements.
- **Separate muscles** instead of one volume: which thigh muscles, and where their shapes come from.
- **Tissue properties:** stiffness, damping and density of muscle, fat and skin from published measurements, in place of values chosen by look.
- **Body shape types** (ampulheta, pêra, trapézio…) as distributions of bone, muscle and fat, with pattern-making measures to check them.

Rafael's films show the body clothed most of the time (2026-11-07). Cloth simulation will come on top of the body later, and it hides part of the skin's detail. The body still comes first.
